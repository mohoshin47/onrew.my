1. Overview and Application Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how OneRewards ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the OneRewards: 2048 Puzzle Game mobile application ("App") and related services.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App. This Policy should be read together with any notices shown inside the App.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the features you use, your device settings, and the third-party services enabled in the App. It may include:

A. Account and sign-in information

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive account information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile image or profile identifier. We use this information to create and manage your account. We do not receive your Google password.

B. Device and advertising information

The App and its advertising or analytics partners may process device-related information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, IP address, diagnostic data, and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), where permitted by your device settings and applicable law.

C. Gameplay, rewards, and activity information

To operate game and reward features, we may process gameplay progress, scores, achievements, daily check-in records, login streaks, XP points, tickets, leaderboard entries, reward balances, redemption requests, and related timestamps or transaction records.

D. Support communications

If you contact our support team, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, account identifier, and the contents of your message.

3. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts and sign-in sessions.
  • Saving gameplay progress, scores, achievements, XP, tickets, and reward balances.
  • Operating leaderboards, daily activities, and reward or redemption features.
  • Reviewing reward requests and detecting suspicious activity, abuse, or fraud.
  • Providing customer support and responding to user requests.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, securing, and improving the App.
  • Showing and measuring advertisements, including rewarded ads, where enabled.
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our terms and policies.

We do not permit service providers to use information for purposes unrelated to their services to us, subject to their applicable terms and law.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain account and reward records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary by data type and purpose.

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information. Some limited records may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or resolving outstanding transactions.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the OneRewards App and look for the account deletion option under Account or Settings, if available.
  2. Alternatively, email support@onrew.my using the email address associated with your account.
  3. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request. Include your registered account email or user ID so we can identify the account. Do not send your password.

We will verify the request where necessary and process eligible deletion requests within 7 business days. If additional time is legally permitted and required, we will inform you. Deleting your account may permanently remove your game progress, XP, tickets, and reward history.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us at support@onrew.my. We will review the request and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

Where local law sets a higher age threshold or requires parental consent, we will follow the applicable requirements.
